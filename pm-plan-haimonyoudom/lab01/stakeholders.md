# Stakeholder priorities: Realtime Quiz

The **Head of the IT department (S01)** and the **data protection officer/registrar (S09)** deserve early attention because both have high influence and high interest in the project's outcome. The sponsor funds and authorises the work and needs evidence at the week-7 MVP demo and week-14 pilot; the data protection stakeholder needs to confirm what student information is stored, who can access it, and how long it is retained. The data protection officer/registrar is a **veto stakeholder**: if the required student-data approval is not obtained, the team must not run the pilot with real student data.

The supervising lecturer/project manager (S02) also needs regular contact with the sponsor to keep scope, schedule, and the $200 cash cap aligned. IT operations and the campus network administrator should be engaged before the pilot so that deployment, backups, monitoring, and WebSocket connectivity are tested before go-live.

Ratings are initial planning estimates and should be confirmed with the people concerned. H = high, M = medium, L = low.
